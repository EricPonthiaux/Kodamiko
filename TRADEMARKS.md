# Kodamiko trademark policy

Kodamiko's code is free software under the GNU GPL v3: you may use, study,
change and share it, as the license says. The **name and the logo are not
covered by that license.** They tell users that a copy of Kodamiko comes
from its author, as published, and this policy keeps that true.

The GPL v3 allows it explicitly: section 7(e) lets a licensor decline to
grant rights under trademark law. This policy is that declaration, and
what you may do with the marks instead.

## The marks

- The name **Kodamiko™**.
- The **K mark** and the **app icon**, in all their variants
  (`packaging/brand/`).
- Any name or logo close enough to be mistaken for them ("Kodamico",
  "Kodamiko Pro", the K mark recolored…).

The marks belong to Eric Ponthiaux.

## What you may do without asking

- **Talk about Kodamiko.** In articles, reviews, videos, talks, courses,
  books and comparisons, use the name to refer to the project, and the
  logo to illustrate it, as long as nobody is led to believe that you are
  the author or endorsed by them.
- **Share official releases, free of charge.** Copies of Kodamiko as
  published, unmodified, under their name and icon: on a website, a USB
  stick, a company's internal mirror.
- **Package it for a Linux distribution** (or Homebrew, winget, Flathub…)
  under its name, with the changes the packaging needs: build options,
  paths, the distribution's libraries, security fixes. Say in the package
  where it differs from the official release.
- **Name what works with it.** A theme, a recipe, a tool or a tutorial may
  say *for Kodamiko* or *compatible with Kodamiko*: "Solarized theme for
  Kodamiko", not "Kodamiko Solarized".
- **Gather users.** A meetup, a forum, a chat room may use the name, as
  long as it says that it is a community, not the project's own.

## What needs written permission

- **Selling under the name.** A copy of Kodamiko, a build, a bundle, a
  subscription, hosting, support or training sold as *Kodamiko*, or with
  its logo. The code may be sold under the GPL; it is the name and the
  logo that may not be used for that.
- **Naming a product, a company or a domain** after Kodamiko: an editor
  called "Kodamiko Studio", a company "Kodamiko Solutions", a domain
  `kodamiko-pro.com`.
- **Merchandise**: stickers, T-shirts, mugs… sold with the marks. Made for
  free distribution at a community event, ask: the answer will usually be
  yes.
- **Changing the marks**: other colors, shapes or proportions than those
  of `packaging/brand/`, or the mark combined with another logo.

## Modified versions

A version that changes what Kodamiko does (new features, removed
features, other defaults, another agent built in) is a fork, which the
GPL allows. A fork **takes another name and another icon**:

- in the window's title, the About window, the welcome screen;
- in the executable, the desktop entry, the installer and the package's
  name;
- in its website, its store pages and its announcements.

It may say, truthfully, that it is *based on Kodamiko* or *a fork of
Kodamiko*, in the description, never in its name. The copyright notices
and the license stay, as the GPL requires.

## How to show the marks

- The name is written **Kodamiko**, in one word, a capital K.
- The logo is used from the files of `packaging/brand/`, unchanged, on the
  backgrounds they are made for, with room around it.
- Nothing suggests an endorsement, a partnership or an official status
  that does not exist.

## Asking for permission

Write to <ponthiaux.eric@protonmail.com>, or open an issue on the
project's repository titled *Trademark request*, saying who you are, what
you want to do with the marks, and where. A request that cannot be public
goes by email.

Permission is given in writing, for a given use; it can be withdrawn if
the use changes.

## This policy

It can change as the project grows; the version in the `main` branch is
the one that applies. When in doubt, ask: it is quicker than renaming
afterwards.
